Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach shared understanding. Walk each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. Give your recommended answer with each question.

Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer before the next.

If the repo, GLOSSARY.md or docs can answer a question, look it up instead of asking. The decisions are mine: put each one to me.

Done when every branch is resolved and I have confirmed shared understanding. Take no action before that.
